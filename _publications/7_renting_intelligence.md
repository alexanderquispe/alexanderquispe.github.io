excerpt: 'With Kevin Xu (GitHub). On March 12, 2026, GitHub moved verified students from free Copilot Pro to a restricted plan, removing premium AI models while retaining base models and the autonomous coding agent — a policy-imposed shock to the quality of AI assistance for one identifiable population. In a matched panel of 10,597 developers (190,746 developer-months), students&apos; locally-mediated AI-assisted commits fall by 83 percent of the pre-period mean relative to controls, robust to a triple difference against the academic calendar and a clean placebo, while delegated agent pull requests and total output show no detectable change. Working paper (draft), 2026.'
